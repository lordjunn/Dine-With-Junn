## 2026-10-01 (Thursday)

### BBQ Pork Wanton Mee Small [Restoran JC Plus]
- Price: RM 8.50
- Meal: Breakfast
- Image: ""

First of the month, and somehow it's another client meeting. I woke at 6, gamed, cleaned myself, and arrived at 7:15am or so. 

Some stalls aren't opened yet, and Gemini suggested something on the "lighter" end. It even said my previous meal here is a fine choice, but why would I eat something that I had tried previously. 

- Ordered small as I'm anticipating eating a bigger lunch
- 8? pieces of BBQ Pork, they taste fine at first but eating more I feel the fried version is better.
- 3 Wantons, their taste could be better meat wise
- 4 Pork Lard pieces, they taste lardy (aka good)
- Vege amount is... small, to say the least.
- (For those who may wonder why not mamak when it's cheaper, let's blame Gemini)

I exited the restaurant at 7:43am. I arrived to client area at 8:53am, which is a little too early (even went to check my tyre pressure & cleaned my car a bit). Maybe I overestimated the time I'd get stuck in traffic or navigation hell (Waze said 50mins or so yesterday, so I went for a 1.5x - 2x buffer). Guess that's a lesson learnt for next time.

Post-meeting and I must say: "The future is not about coding, but understanding requirements/customers/talking" (something along the lines, per CEO). All went well I can say.

My water is going to run out, and I was thinking of Gepuk, since I never had it in... since 2026-06-22 as mentioned in 2026-09-24, so might as well.

Kai (Whatsapp): "ng u kena scam ke; u go eat mixed rice get more than that; either way ur ass got scammed harder than [censored]"

## 2026-10-15 (Thursday)

### Char Siew Wanton Mee Small [Yi Niang Kopitiam]
- Price: RM 9.00
- Meal: Breakfast
- Image: ""

Another outing, another client onsite (I mean I'd take occasional long ish trips over short consistent office suffering any day). This time it's at 11am, so I woke up an hour later.

Initially I was planning Publika, but I thought it was a little crowded & hot, so diverted to Restoran Bandar Kinrara, which was closed despite Google saying it'd be open at 8. Went to park nearby Publika anyways, and walked towards the LRT. 
Saw a mamak, thought it'd be normal, then went to Yi Niang as I had it before once but seemingly forgotten how it's like nowadays.

Noodles took a bit of time (8:20? - 8:36), must be batch cooking since people who came later than me arrived more or less the same time.

- Char Siew is slightly tough initially, but flavourful
- Same goes to the soup, flavourful & finished it
- Wanton is good, better than JC Plus, 3 of them
- Vege side it's quite some stems, but edible otherwise
- 5 slices of Char Siew & 2 pieces of Wanton remains as I finished the small noodles.

Left the restaurant at 8:50am, boarded LRT at 9, and reached around 10... which is still an hour early. I should've ordered big. Now to hope lunch is good.

## 2026-10-27 (Tuesday)

### [Mixed Rice] [Restoran Bandar Kinrara]
- Price: RM 9.00
- Meal: Lunch
- Image: ""

I usually have this back in the day, so why not give it a try with my adult money?
While queuing there's a slight crowd/queue, which means good usually... until I took the tong to clamp the chicken. USA would invade it unannounced. (There's not much meat selection sadly)

- Chicken: thigh. Board said it costs 5.5 or so. Tastes ok, as expected.
- Vege: kangkung. 1 tongful (kiap). Chili, garlic, oil.
- Rice: a single scoop, given by the foriegn worker.
- No sauce as there's nothing worthy of being scooped.
- Expecting 9.50 as 1 vege 1 rice costs like 4 according to the board, but I'll take the 9.

I should've went to S11 which is nearby. I'd get free flow soup & tea. (Per Kai & co in group chat, I can't tell if my sense of price is off, or here is "expensive". All I could hook on is Dapo, which has the cheapest lunch meals in terms of price, fullness, satisfaction)
Or maybe even something that's made to order instead of fearing an invasion of oils.

Meeting proceeded to be cancelled after dealing with the lobby receptionist & shitting myself in the meeting floor toilets. On the bright side, a car ride with the CEO, no LRT needed (was kinda raining).
