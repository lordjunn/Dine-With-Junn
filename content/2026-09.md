## 2026-09-24 (Thursday)

### Minced Meat Zhar Yoke Mee [Restoran JC Plus]
- Price: RM 10.00
- Meal: Lunch
- Image: images/2026/September/2026, September, image 4.png

So somehow work was essentially remote, hence why I have like 0 meals to log, until today when parents decided to go out, just the 2 of them. I got told to settle my own lunch. 

A debate between 3 restaurants took up some planning time at 12:
- Jasir Bistro (local mamak, nearest + free parking, "boring but works")
- Anjung Mahsuri (new food place, never tried before, but considered semi unknown territory)
- Shops further south (known area, usually good, paid + slightly hard to find parking)

Was debating on Jasir vs down south, wheel pointed to Anjung, then Jasir. I decided to gamble, and found parking a lil further from the restaurant. Paid 53 cents to be a law abiding citizen, and to prevent losing 1000 of it instead.
Chose this stall as it seemed the safest to me + had people queueing up waiting for it, means it should be decent.

- 9 cuts of a strip of fried pork, not too juicy but servicable for local food
- Minced meat, they're tasteful I'd say
- Some vege
- Soup, is fine
- Of course, it can't compare to peak. But peak has been missing for quite some time.

Would I come again? I guess? But maybe I'd try something else for a change. Speaking of which I should've just gepuk-ed.
I would've been more full (last I had gepuk was 2026-06-22) despite it costs like 20% more. It isn't a food my family would eat.
