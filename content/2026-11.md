## 2026-11-13 (Friday)

### Mixed Rice [S11 Puncak Jalil]
- Price: RM 10.20
- Meal: Lunch
- Image: ""

Family decided to go out, just the 3 of them. Lunch is then settled by myself as paraphrase: "I'm an adult now". Would I like to go out? No I have a job to deal with. Damn.

Small debate whether I should instant noodles (it was slightly raining), Jasir Bistro (free parking), PUJ commercial area (paid parking), or Giant Kinrara (free 2h parking). Of course, the safest option was chosen, which is mixed rice. Also a way to gauge prices (for the 2026-10-27 me).

- Butter chicken: 5.00. 22 bite sized pieces or so. 6 remained when I finished the rice.
- Vege: 2.60. Around 1 scoop then a bit more mini scoops
- Rice: 2.60. 1 big scoop
- Chicken curry for the sauce, 2-3 scoops.
- Liquids: 2 cups of chinese tea, 1 bowl of soup

Herrscher calls it "kinda expensive"? But I guess I may have took too much meat (in their eyes). Ma zi if I took this much probably could near it, even with the card.
Looking back, I guess they seem similar (assuming factoring in tea)? But to truly gauge I'd need to take the exact same type of food, ignoring portion.
I mean, it's cheaper than gepuk, and the drinks here are unlimited. Theoretically speaking, even meat & vege is also unlimited if one digs into the soup. Would've chilled longer & even bought my work laptop here if it wasn't for the parking as it has A/C here.
